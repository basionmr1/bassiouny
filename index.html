Specialized care, wherever you are

The first months matter most

Prof. Bassiouny offers online consultations for parents of newborns and young children, with special attention to babies who spent time in a neonatal intensive care unit.

Each case is reviewed in advance from the baby's records: the pregnancy and birth details, the NICU course, discharge summary, growth measurements, laboratory results and imaging reports.

Whether you need guidance after discharge, help understanding your baby's growth and development, or a second opinion on a complex condition, you will get a clear plan for the next steps.
